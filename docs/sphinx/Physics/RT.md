# Radiative Transfer

The ability to solve radiative transfer problems in Cholla is under development.

## Optically-Thin Variable Eddington Tensor (OTVET) Scheme (in dev)

Nick Gnedin implemented his OTVET radiative transfer scheme (see, e.g., [Gnedin & Abel 2001](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2001NewA....6..437G/abstract)). 

## M1 Scheme (in dev)

An M1 scheme for Cholla is under development, building from Nick Gnedin's M1 implementation in his Altair code.  The original M1 reference is [C. D. Levermore, 1984](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1984JQSRT..31..149L/abstract).
